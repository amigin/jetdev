From whiteboard to production.

Eight to sixteen weeks, four phases. Nothing ships until the numbers and a person agree.

  1. Phase 1

    Model the market

    Instruments, venues and order states become types. An invalid trade cannot be written down, so it cannot be sent.

  2. Phase 2

    Optimize the hot path

    Profiled, not guessed: lock-free structures, NUMA-aware memory, cache-line discipline, SIMD where it pays.

  3. Phase 3

    Backtest and benchmark

    Real order books, replayed. Criterion runs in CI, and a commit that breaks the budget fails the build.

  4. Phase 4

    Ship and observe

    A canary next to the old system and two weeks of shadow traffic. Cutover only after p99 and correctness pass human review.
